Driven by Industry

Hefei Pulian Automated Instruments Co., Ltd. was established in 2005 with a registered capital of RMB 10.5 million. We are ISO 9001-2015 certified and specialize in the R&D, manufacturing, and export of precision flow measurement instruments.

Our products serve demanding sectors including petrochemical, military, metallurgy, and marine engineering, with active exports to Russia and CIS countries.
